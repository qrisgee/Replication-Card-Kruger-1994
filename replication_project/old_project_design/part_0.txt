1.What is the causal question?
	- How does a change in minimum wage impact the economic activity of relevant industries (i.e. industries that actually hire minimum wage individuals
	-In this replication assignment, the relevant industry we are observing are fast-food restaurants
	-In this replication assignment, we define economic activity by observing employment levels
2. What is the treatment?
	- The treatment is the increase in minimum wage from $4.25 to $5.05 implemented by New Jersey on April 1992
3.What defines the treated and control groups?
	-The control group is Pennsylvania; where the minimum wage did not change
	-The treated group is New Jersey; where the minimum wage did change
4.What is the outcome variable?
	- The outcome variable will be the difference in employment of fast-food chains in these two states
5.Why would a simple post-policy comparison be misleading?
	-Exogenous variables and concurrent policy changes could've influenced economic activity
	-Time lags: With a simple post-policy comparison, it's difficult to identify a reference point that identifies when the implementation of a policy actually starts to have an impact
